Sokutsu — on-device interpreter and meeting notes for face-to-face and online meetings

REQUIREMENTS
  A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13.3 or later.
  Listening to the other side of an online meeting needs macOS 14.2 or later.
  About 5 GB free disk space (models are about 3.4 GB). 16 GB of memory recommended (8 GB works, more slowly).

INSTALL
  1. Drag Sokutsu onto "Applications" in this window.
  2. The first time you open it (the app is not notarized by Apple, so this is needed once):
     - macOS 14 or earlier: in Applications, Control-click (right-click) Sokutsu, choose Open, then Open.
     - macOS 15 or later: open Sokutsu once and click Done on the warning. Then open
       System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to '"Sokutsu" was blocked…',
       click "Open Anyway", enter your password and click Open.
  After that it opens normally.

FIRST-TIME SETUP
  On first launch Sokutsu downloads its speech, translation and voice models (about 3.4 GB) from their
  official sources (Hugging Face, GitHub). If you close the window, it resumes next time.
  After that it works without a network connection.
  Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use found at ai.google.dev/gemma/terms.

PERMISSIONS
  Microphone: to hear what is said (required).
  Screen & System Audio Recording: only when you choose "Mic + this Mac's sound" for online meetings.
  Calendars / Reminders: only when you press those buttons.

YOUR DATA (all on this Mac; nothing is sent anywhere)
  Settings, transcripts, recordings, glossary, voices, models: ~/Library/Application Support/Sokutsu/
  App logs: ~/Library/Logs/Sokutsu/
  To uninstall, move Sokutsu.app and these two folders to the Trash.

ADVANCED: YOUR OWN TRANSLATION SERVER
  Add to ~/Library/Application Support/Sokutsu/config.json:
    "server_url": "http://<host>:<port>"   (an OpenAI-compatible /v1 server)
    "server_model": "<model id>"          (optional; defaults to the server's first model)
  A "Your server" option then appears. By default everything runs on this Mac.
